My favorite hobby is stargazing, particularly on clear, moonless nights far away from city lights. There's something incredibly humbling and awe-inspiring about pointing a telescope up at the night sky and exploring the cosmos. Whether I'm trying to find the faint, fuzzy outline of the Andromeda Galaxy, tracking the moons of Jupiter, or simply identifying familiar constellations, the silence and the scale of the universe offer a perfect escape from the daily grind. It's a blend of relaxation, scientific curiosity, and a deep sense of wonder that I don't find in any other activity.
