The annual coding competition drew participants from across the globe, with the final round focusing on three critical areas: **Algorithm Efficiency**, which tested the speed of the code; **Data Structure Implementation**, which assessed the effective use of lists, trees, and graphs; and finally, **Security Vulnerability Patches**, a timed challenge to identify and fix flaws in a sample application. Each **element** was weighted equally, ensuring the winner demonstrated a truly well-rounded skill set, not just mastery in a single domain.
