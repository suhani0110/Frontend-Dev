About Us

We are a small studio focused on front-end craftsmanship. Our approach emphasizes structure, spacing, and accessibility — every pixel is deliberate.

Using the CSS box model, we create interfaces that are predictable, robust, and responsive. Margin, padding, borders, and outlines are used to guide the user’s eye and improve readability.
